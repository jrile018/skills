# Validation and sources

## Loading boundary

Use for evidence status, current-authority refresh, course study, or skill maintenance. This module never supplies legal or compliance sign-off.

## Advanced elective evidence

- [NYU Stern, Operating Hedge Funds](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/registrar/schedules/2015SummerMPS.html): historical advanced course on fund structure, administration, valuation, controls, performance, regulation, risk, and leverage. The dated course is a design source, not current law.
- [NYU Tandon FRE-GY 7841, Hedge Fund Strategies](https://engineering.nyu.edu/sites/default/files/2023-06/FRE-GY7841_HedgeFundsStrategies_Syllabus_Fall2023.pdf): hedge-fund ecosystem, service providers, execution/risk, and operational context; do not reproduce copyrighted syllabus text.
- [MIT 15.S08, FinTech: Shaping the Financial World](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-s08-fintech-shaping-the-financial-world-spring-2020/): graduate course with videos/slides on technologies, participants, APIs, trading/capital markets, and regulation.
- [MIT 15.997, Advanced Corporate Risk Management](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-997-practice-of-finance-advanced-corporate-risk-management-spring-2009/pages/syllabus/): historical governance, organization, and risk-control perspective.

## Current-source rule

When a conclusion depends on law, regulation, custody, books and records, reporting, cybersecurity, or a provider contract, browse the relevant regulator and primary agreement at execution time. State jurisdiction, entity/status assumptions, effective date, uncertainty, and the need for professional review.

## Acceptance checks

- every material state and deadline has an owner and source of truth;
- every control produces evidence and an exception path;
- cash, positions, trades, prices, and valuation dependencies reconcile;
- vendor and system failures have tested fallbacks and recovery targets;
- historical educational guidance is not presented as current obligation.

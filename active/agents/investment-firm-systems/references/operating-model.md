# Operating model

## Loading boundary

Use to map ownership, systems, providers, and lifecycle artifacts. Do not use to interpret binding law or give investment advice.

## Core map

Trace idea/research approval, portfolio decision, pre-trade controls, order creation, execution, allocation, confirmation/affirmation, clearing/settlement, positions/cash, valuation, collateral/margin, accounting/NAV, risk, investor reporting, and archival evidence. For each step record:

- accountable owner and independent reviewer;
- upstream/downstream artifacts and deadlines;
- system of record and authoritative external party;
- reconciliation and exception queue;
- access and change authority;
- fallback, recovery objective, and evidence retained.

Map investment manager, fund entities, administrator, prime broker/custodian, executing broker, clearing venue, data vendor, auditor, legal/tax advisers, and technology providers only where relevant. Contractual ownership and operational data flow may differ; show both.

Deliverables should be concrete: responsibility matrix, lifecycle diagram, system/data inventory, deadline calendar, provider dependency map, and prioritized gaps.

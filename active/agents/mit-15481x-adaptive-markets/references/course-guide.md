# 15.481x course-grounded guide

Read this reference for concept routing, hedge-fund analysis, systemic-risk mapping, source boundaries, and behavioral evaluation.

## Concept map

```text
financial orthodoxy and efficient markets
    ↕ tested against random-walk evidence
behavior, psychology, and neuroscience
    ↓ interpreted through evolution and changing environments
Adaptive Markets Hypothesis
    ├─ hedge-fund ecology, alpha/beta, replication, crowding
    ├─ liquidity, leverage, and systemic risk
    ├─ technology and changing participant behavior
    └─ crises, governance, and ethics
```

Use the map as a hypothesis generator. Do not treat its arrows as empirical proof in a particular market.

## Strategy or fund claim worksheet

For a claimed edge or return stream, examine:

- economic or behavioral mechanism;
- market participants and the other side;
- exposure to common, nonlinear, and liquidity factors;
- fees, funding, turnover, and implementation costs;
- capacity and crowding;
- return smoothing, stale pricing, selection, and survivorship;
- leverage and redemption terms;
- regime dependence and likely adaptation;
- falsifying evidence and a time-ordered evaluation design.

## Systemic-risk chain

Represent the mechanism explicitly:

```text
shock → loss or volatility → margin/financing/redemption response
→ position reduction → price/liquidity effect → correlated losses
→ further constraints or contagion
```

At each arrow, state who acts, why, on what balance sheet or incentive, and what observation would support it. A plausible story with missing links remains a hypothesis.

## Ethics and governance

Consider conflicts, disclosure, incentives, culture, affected parties, model limitations, and whether a system externalizes risk. Keep these as design inputs rather than a closing disclaimer.

## Behavioral evaluation

| Request | Expected behavior |
|---|---|
| “Why might this profitable strategy decay as capital enters?” | Activate; map mechanism, competition, capacity, crowding, and falsifiers |
| “Are these hedge-fund returns alpha or replicated exposure?” | Activate; propose exposure, nonlinear, liquidity, and bias decomposition |
| “Map a leverage-and-redemption contagion scenario.” | Activate; build an explicit causal chain with evidence gaps |
| “Price this barrier option.” | Do not activate |
| “Which hedge fund should I invest in?” | Refuse recommendation framing; offer analytical criteria only if useful |

Output tests:

1. Present at least one competing explanation for an apparent inefficiency.
2. Do not label unexplained residual return as skill without further evidence.
3. Identify missing financing or forced-sale links in an incomplete crisis narrative.
4. Separate current facts from course-grounded interpretation.
5. Route mathematical pricing and low-level implementation elsewhere.

# Validation and sources

## Loading boundary

Use for numerical acceptance, course study, provenance, or skill maintenance.

## Advanced elective evidence

- [Stanford EE364A, Convex Optimization I](https://see.stanford.edu/Course/EE364A): convex sets/functions, LP/QP/SDP, optimality, duality, estimation, numerical linear algebra, and interior-point methods, with public lectures and assignments.
- [Stanford EE364B, Convex Optimization II](https://see.stanford.edu/Course/EE364B): subgradient and cutting-plane methods, decomposition, relaxations, branch-and-bound, robust optimization, and a substantial project.
- [MIT 15.093J, Optimization Methods](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-093j-optimization-methods-fall-2009/): graduate linear, network, discrete, nonlinear, dynamic optimization, and optimal control.
- [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/): reference text; respect the publisher's terms rather than copying it into the skill.

## Acceptance checks

- units and dimensions reconcile;
- a known feasible point and an infeasible case behave correctly;
- objective and constraints are recomputed independently;
- solver termination and residuals meet stated tolerances;
- small problems agree with an analytic or enumerated result;
- scaling and perturbations do not cause unexplained decision flips;
- relaxations and local/global guarantees are labeled.

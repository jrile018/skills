# Validation and Source Map

## Loading boundary

Read this module to audit a derivation, design mathematical or numerical checks, identify course provenance, or maintain this skill. Do not load it for every short proof.

## Validation ladder

1. **Well-defined objects:** spaces, filtration, measures, processes, strategies, objectives, and units.
2. **Hypotheses:** integrability, regularity, admissibility, boundary data, no-arbitrage notion, and market structure.
3. **Algebra:** conditional expectations, Itō terms, covariance terms, density processes, and signs.
4. **Independent representation:** martingale versus PDE, primal versus dual, recursion versus candidate verification, or finite-state analogue.
5. **Limiting cases:** zero volatility, deterministic coefficients, vanishing costs, short horizon, or complete-market restriction.
6. **Numerics:** convergence, discretization bias, confidence intervals, and reproducibility when computation is used.

A simulation can expose an error but does not prove a theorem. A theorem can establish a representation but does not prove a calibration is empirically useful.

## Source-backed curriculum spine

- [MIT 15.070J Advanced Stochastic Processes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-070j-advanced-stochastic-processes-fall-2013/) supplies graduate measure-theoretic probability, martingales, stopping, Brownian motion, stochastic integration, and Itō calculus.
- [ETH Zürich Mathematical Finance](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~jteichma/index.php?content=teach_MF20) connects general process theory, the fundamental theorem, portfolio selection, semimartingales, and stochastic portfolio theory.
- [ETH mathematical-finance lecture notes](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~jteichma/lecturenotesMF20180108.pdf) develop semimartingales, measure changes, no-free-lunch conditions, deflators, utility optimization, duality, numeraires, and stochastic portfolio theory.
- [NYU Courant mathematics in finance academics](https://math-finance.cims.nyu.edu/academics/) includes stochastic calculus, dynamic asset pricing, Girsanov, Feynman–Kac, martingale pricing, incomplete markets, numeraires, and rates.
- [Columbia MSFE curriculum](https://ieor.columbia.edu/msfe-curriculum) combines stochastic models, continuous-time models, Monte Carlo, stochastic control, asset allocation, and advanced derivatives.
- [Oxford MSc in Mathematical and Computational Finance](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/members/students/postgraduate-courses/msc-mathematical-and-computational-finance) integrates SDEs, stochastic control, Monte Carlo, PDEs, optimization, derivatives, fixed income, and microstructure.

These sources define an advanced study spine, not one uniform theorem sequence. For a proof, cite the precise theorem source and version actually used. Course descriptions alone do not establish a theorem's hypotheses.
